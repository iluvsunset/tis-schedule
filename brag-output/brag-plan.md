# Brag Plan: TIS Schedule

## What is this app?
TIS Schedule is a real-time academic operating system and timetable web platform for The International School (Khối THCS & THPT), replacing static PDF timetables with live classroom kiosks, period telemetry, sub-second Google Sheets synchronization, and automated evening preparation reminders.

## The angle
Deep thinking inquiry meets high-octane editorial motion graphics:
"Every school day is a complex distributed coordination problem. 700+ students. 32 physical rooms. 10 periods a day. Why do we still trust crushed JPEG screenshots on Zalo to navigate physical reality?"
TIS Schedule re-engineers time into a living, responsive grid. Zero guesswork. Zero friction.

## Anti-Slop Directive
Strict adherence to user constraint:
- NO AI slop.
- NO floating pill cards, generic rounded badge chips, or sparkle emojis.
- High-contrast Swiss editorial typography, raw architectural grids, laser-crisp geometric dividers, technical monospace telemetry, authentic live school data (Room 504, Class 11.1-TN, Sinh Học - Thầy Công, English - Ms. P Anh).

## Hook (first 7.5 seconds)
A stark black frame violently interrupted by massive kinetic typography:
"WHERE ARE YOU SUPPOSED TO BE RIGHT NOW?"
A rapid-fire statistical breakdown: "700 STUDENTS. 32 CLASSROOMS. 1 CRUSHED JPEG ON ZALO."
A high-precision millisecond countdown clock hits 07:39:59... 07:40:00 ICT. The morning bell rings.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Telemetry Kiosk (Room 504)**: Real-time classroom radar showing remaining minutes, progress indicator, active subject (Sinh Học - Thầy Công), and next class in queue.
2. **The Timeline Re-engineered**: Linear period progression (07:40 Good Morning -> 08:30 Sinh Học -> 09:15 Recess -> 09:30 Science -> 10:20 English) breaking down the day with mathematical clarity.
3. **The Multi-Dimensional School Matrix**: Dynamic bird's-eye view of all grades (6.1 to 12.1) across all weekdays.
4. **The Live Cloud Reconciliation Engine**: Teacher substitute at 07:35 AM? Direct sync with Google Sheets edge-reconciled in under 200ms.
5. **The Evening Prep Loop**: 19:30 automatic reminder triggers: "Chuẩn bị sách vở cho ngày mai."
